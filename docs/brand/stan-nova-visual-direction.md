## Purpose and control boundary

This record captures direction communicated through two Stan and Nova concepts
without turning either concept into production authority. It is an interpretation
brief for review, not a character sheet, asset specification, licence grant, or
approval to trace, redraw, animate, train on, ship, or publish the concepts.

The source images must pass the [concept-reference intake gate](reference/stan-nova/README.md)
before they are added to the repository. Until then, artists and developers must
not derive production assets from descriptions in this document. After intake,
the images still remain concept references unless Michael gives explicit written
canonical approval.

## Direction visible in the concepts

The items below are **approved only as topics the canonical review must decide**.
Their depicted execution, exact geometry, colour values, proportions, materials,
and styling remain non-authoritative.

| Trait | Shared directional signal to preserve for review | Non-authoritative details not to infer |
|---|---|---|
| Relative size | Stan and Nova should read immediately as a coordinated pair, with a deliberate size relationship. | Which character is taller/larger, the exact height ratio, head-to-body ratio, and whether scale changes by pose or scene. |
| Silhouettes | Each companion needs a distinct, readable silhouette while retaining a related visual language. | Exact anatomy, body mass, ear/hair shapes, limb proportions, footwear, garment cut, and turnaround profile. |
| Face treatment | Faces should be legible, approachable, and emotionally clear at family-app scale. | Eye construction, pupils/highlights, brows, nose/muzzle treatment, mouth shape, facial markings, texture, and expression model. |
| White / gold / purple palette | White, gold, and purple form the intended shared palette family and should support pair recognition. | Colour values, character ownership of each colour, dominance ratios, gradients, metallic finish, glow, contrast tokens, and accessibility-safe combinations. |
| Emblems | A chest or costume emblem can function as a shared identity cue. | Emblem artwork, letterform/symbol, placement, scale, dimensionality, glow, and whether Stan and Nova use matching or distinct marks. |
| Stan's cape | A cape is a candidate signature element for Stan and supports a protective, confident silhouette. | Length, colour arrangement, fastening, material, physics, use in every context, and safety/animation treatment. |
| Nova's headset | A headset is a candidate signature element for Nova and supports an attentive, connected role. | Earpiece/boom configuration, side, colour, lights, microphone shape, technical function, recording implication, and use in every context. |
| Emotional tone | The pair should feel warm, reassuring, capable, optimistic, and collaborative rather than intimidating or cold. | A fixed personality, voice, pose library, relationship hierarchy, or promise that the characters possess authority over family members. |
| Family-safe presentation | Presentation should be non-sexualised, non-violent, age-appropriate, inclusive, and reassuring; expressions and poses should support trust without coercion. | Safety certification, suitability for every age/market, or permission to use surveillance cues, weapons, fear, shame, deceptive authority, or manipulative urgency. |

The concepts do not override the existing product roles: Stan supports
safety-minded guidance, permissions, trusted observations, and human control;
Nova provides warm family support only within approved least-privilege contexts.
Visual motifs must not imply powers, monitoring, recording, or decision authority
the product does not have.

## Inconsistencies requiring explicit resolution

The two concept images are not available as verified source files in this
repository, so a defensible image-by-image comparison cannot yet be recorded.
The inconsistency audit is therefore **open**, not silently resolved. At review,
compare the concepts side by side and record differences in at least these areas:

1. Stan-to-Nova height, width, and head/body proportions.
2. Overall silhouette, anatomy, garment shapes, hands/feet, and pose language.
3. Face construction, eye style, facial features/markings, and expression.
4. Allocation, saturation, and dominance of white, gold, and purple.
5. Emblem symbol, location, scale, colour, and shared-versus-individual treatment.
6. Stan's cape length, attachment, exterior/interior colours, and material.
7. Nova's headset form, placement/side, microphone, lights, and implied function.
8. Rendering style, outline weight, dimensionality, lighting, texture, and finish.
9. Emotional age, confidence, warmth, energy, and pair relationship.
10. Any family-safety concern, including aggression, sexualisation, frightening
    features, unsafe accessories, coercive cues, or implied surveillance.

Do not average conflicting details, choose the more polished concept, or treat
repetition across two concepts as approval. Each unresolved difference remains a
design decision for the Founder.
