# Stan and Nova concept-reference intake

**Status: HOLD — no image is approved for repository intake or production use.**

This directory is the dedicated evidence area for the two Stan and Nova concept
images discussed in the visual-direction record. The images themselves are not
stored here yet. No original, unwatermarked export or usage-rights evidence was
available in the repository at the time this record was created, so adding a
watermarked copy, reconstructing an image, or asserting permission would create
false provenance.

## Required intake gate

Before either image is committed, the asset owner must provide all of the
following:

1. The original unwatermarked export at its native dimensions (not a screenshot,
   chat preview, upscaled copy, or recreated derivative).
2. The creator or rights-holder's name and contact or other traceable identity.
3. Written evidence that KYREC may retain the file and use it internally as a
   concept reference, including any limits on modification, publication,
   commercial use, sublicensing, territory, duration, or attribution.
4. The source/tool and creation date, plus disclosure of any third-party or
   generative-AI inputs whose terms may affect use.
5. Confirmation that recognizable people, protected third-party characters,
   logos, fonts, stock elements, or other licensed material are absent or are
   separately cleared.
6. Michael's explicit decision on whether the images remain internal-only and,
   separately, whether either design becomes canonical.

Legal or brand review should resolve ambiguous ownership or licence terms; this
record is not a legal conclusion.
