# KYREC Privacy & Compliance Gate — Codex Skill

## Owner
Vera — Standards & Operations, with qualified advisers where required.

## Use when
Use for accounts, identity, family roles, children, location, tracking, emotional/wellbeing data, financial patterns, memory, uploads, third-party APIs, analytics, messaging or consequential actions.

## Company rules
- Family first. Useful technology. Human control. Honest progress.
- Never invent legal conclusions, compliance status, approvals or evidence.
- Preserve original evidence before changing or deleting it.
- Public brand stays alive. Technical internals stay private.
- This skill does not replace Australian legal advice or qualified privacy/security review.

## Privacy instructions
1. Identify what data is collected, why, who can see it, where it flows and how long it remains.
2. Collect only what the feature needs.
3. Consent must be understandable, purpose-linked, visible and revocable.
4. Location and family-role access require misuse-resistant design, role/time limits, access history and rapid revocation where appropriate.
5. Raw private conversations are not durable trusted memory by default.
6. Do not infer sensitive facts merely because signals could support them.
7. Companions receive only permitted context.
8. Consequential actions need stronger human approval.
9. Identify third parties/processors and their data access.
10. Escalate questions requiring a privacy lawyer, regulator guidance, security specialist or other qualified professional.

## Family Guardian high-risk areas
Give extra scrutiny to:
- child accounts
- shared-care and multi-household access
- location and background tracking
- safe zones and journey sharing
- emotional/wellbeing signals
- Family Points and financial patterns
- family memory/history
- uploads containing private household information
- account separation and revocation after relationship/household changes
